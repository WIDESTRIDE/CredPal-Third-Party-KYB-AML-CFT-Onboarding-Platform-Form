    Direct shareholders Required

    Everyone (person or company) who holds shares in your business directly. If a shareholder is a company, tell us who owns that company in "Owned by".

    Ultimate beneficial owners Required

    The natural persons who own 5% or more, or who control the business in any other way. Trace through any companies until you reach real people.
